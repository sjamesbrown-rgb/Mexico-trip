SMA practical tips
→Cabs in SMA are about 100 pesos. Ask the driver first. Leave a small tip. Don't leave your phone in the cab.
→Cab to Casa Mel: "Next to Hotel Villa Santa Mónica, Parque Juárez."
→Luciano has a phone number for cabs if you need one.
→Avoid large purses & flashy jewelry. Don't hook your purse on the back of your chair — easily lifted.
→Weather May: Warm dry days (75–82°F), cool nights (50s). Bring a light jacket.
→Altitude: SMA 6,200ft, CDMX 7,350ft. Drink water, take it easy day 1.
Money & phone
→Use a "no foreign exchange fee" ATM card. ATMs on the square exchange easily.
→When ATM asks "accept conversion rate?" → DECLINE. Let your bank convert.
→Turn off "roaming" on your phone to avoid data charges. Free WiFi is everywhere.
→Carry pesos for cabs, markets, tips. Cards work at most restaurants but not all.
Date format
In Mexico, dates are written day/month/year. So January 5 = 05/01, not 01/05.
